本清单保留归档的 [v10 设计记录](../examples/v0.2.0/slb-webinar-2026-11-25/Seminar_Writing_Design_and_Sources_v10.md) 中全部 **40 个独立外链**，按外部 skills、论文、数据代码、字体素材、设计写作参考分类。原文保留逐页用途与历史决策；此处作为仓库资源索引，不把外部资源变为安装依赖，也不声称独立核验所有文献结论或许可。

This catalog preserves all 40 distinct external URLs in the supplied design record. It records sources and their categories; third-party content is not mirrored or made a mandatory dependency.

## External skills / 外部 skills

These are references recorded in the design document, not mandatory dependencies.

- [Elegant Academic Slides](https://github.com/cw-wan/elegant-academic-slides-skill)：保留原始科学论证与作者归属，明确事件图阅读顺序，并对齐 ELT 终端叶节点。
- [Scientific Writing](https://github.com/K-Dense-AI/scientific-agent-skills/blob/main/skills/scientific-writing/SKILL.md)：准确报告指标、单位和实验设置，区分解释与专家标注。
- [Writing Clearly and Concisely](https://github.com/obra/the-elements-of-style/blob/main/skills/writing-clearly-and-concisely/SKILL.md)：用听众能联系到波形的简短表述组织文案。
- [Humanizer](https://github.com/blader/humanizer/blob/main/SKILL.md)：去除重复铺垫和防御性限定语。
- [Academic Diagram](https://github.com/plbbl/academic-diagram-skill/blob/main/SKILL.md)：先绘制和检查矢量源图，再插入演示文稿。
